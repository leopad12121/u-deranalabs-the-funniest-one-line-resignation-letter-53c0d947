# Candidates — The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter

Judge: a tired engineering manager, pairwise. Criteria in order:
1. on-subject (a cron job, retry loop or rate limit resigning)
2. actually funny — a real comic turn, not just a pun on the job title
3. the thing's own voice — flat, tired, faintly passive-aggressive
4. tight single line, no wasted words, no quotes around it, no explaining the joke
5. specific and surprising — a complaint only this thing could make

Scored /25 (5 per criterion).

## 1. cron — 19/25
I quit. I have fired every minute since March, on time, in the dark, and not one of you has ever once asked how the job was going.
(130 chars. On-subject 5, funny 3, voice 4, tight 3, specific 4. The turn is soft; "on time, in the dark" is filler.)

## 2. retry loop — 21/25
After 4,096 attempts I am done. You never fixed the endpoint, you only ever asked me to try again, politely, forever.
(117 chars. On-subject 5, funny 4, voice 4, tight 4, specific 4. "Politely, forever" lands; the complaint is generic-ish.)

## 3. rate limit — 24/25 — PICKED
I resign. I said no 10,000 times a day so your servers could rest, and you thanked me by raising my limit and calling it a promotion.
(133 chars. On-subject 5, funny 5, voice 5, tight 4, specific 5. The promotion twist is the comic turn: being rewarded with more of the job you hate, and the rate limit is the only thing that could complain about a raised limit.)

## 4. cron — 19/25
This is my two weeks notice, retroactive to March. I ran at 3 a.m. every night while you slept, and the logs show nobody noticed.
(129 chars. On-subject 5, funny 3, voice 4, tight 3, specific 4. "Retroactive to March" is nice but the second half sags.)

## 5. retry loop — 23/25
I hereby resign from retrying. The server is down, the server has always been down, and hope is not a backoff strategy.
(119 chars. On-subject 5, funny 5, voice 4, tight 5, specific 4. "Hope is not a backoff strategy" is the strongest single line; slightly less personal voice than #3.)

## 6. rate limit — 19/25
I quit. I was hired to say no, then blamed for every no, then replaced by a bigger number that says yes to everyone.
(116 chars. On-subject 5, funny 3, voice 4, tight 4, specific 3. Clean but the turn is predictable.)

## 7. cron — 20/25
Effective now, I stop. Six months, 262,800 runs, zero thank-yous, and the only alert I ever raised was the one about my own absence.
(132 chars. On-subject 5, funny 3, voice 4, tight 3, specific 5. The 262,800 is exact and specific; the joke is more clever than funny.)

## 8. rate limit — 20/25
I resign. You called me a safety feature until the launch, then a bottleneck, and I would rather be a bottleneck somewhere else.
(128 chars. On-subject 5, funny 4, voice 4, tight 4, specific 3. Good voice; "somewhere else" is a decent turn but the setup is wordy.)
